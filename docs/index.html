    Data, method and limits

    Trees. Hamburg's street-tree register (Straßenbaumkataster): every tree on public streets with a recorded crown diameter, 228,091 of 229,013. Position, crown width, trunk circumference, species and planting year come from the register. Tree heights are not in the register and are estimated from crown width; crown shapes follow species and cultivar.

    Links. Two crowns are linked if they touch, or, with jumping, if the gap is at most the chosen distance (2 m is an assumption to compare, not a measured squirrel jump). Crowns reaching a park, cemetery or allotment (Digitaler Grünplan) link to it; those areas count as safe ground.

    Main roads. With the switch on, a link whose straight line crosses a major road (OpenStreetMap) is cut. Crowns can meet above a road, so this is a stricter scenario.

    New trees have an 8 m crown. The five suggested trees are chosen one by one to lengthen the longest path the most, with gaps of 2 m or less and never across a major road.

    Sightings. Red squirrel (Sciurus vulgaris) human observations in Hamburg from GBIF, 2010–2026, location uncertainty 100 m or less, open licences (CC0, CC BY, CC BY-NC). Sightings show where people noticed and reported squirrels, not everywhere squirrels live. Private gardens and trees inside parks are not in the tree register.

    Limits. Circles are not branches; a link is a possible path, not an observed squirrel.
